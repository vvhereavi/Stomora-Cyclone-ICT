STORMORA | CASE STUDY: MYANMAR

The History of Cyclone Nargis

Remembering one of the most devastating natural disasters in Myanmar's history and understanding the importance of cyclone preparedness and community safety.

2 MAY 2008
THE EVENT

Understanding Cyclone Nargis

Cyclone Nargis was a powerful tropical cyclone that struck Myanmar in May 2008. It developed over the Bay of Bengal in late April and gradually strengthened as it moved towards the country's southern coastline.

On 2 May 2008, Cyclone Nargis made landfall in the Ayeyarwady Region of Myanmar. It continued moving inland, affecting the Ayeyarwady Delta and areas around Yangon. The cyclone was particularly destructive because of the storm surge that swept into low-lying delta communities.

Cyclone Nargis became one of the deadliest natural disasters in Myanmar's recorded history. Approximately 140,000 people were reported dead or missing, while around 2.4 million people were severely affected. Its impact left a lasting mark on the country's history.

FOLLOWING MONTHS

Emergency Relief and Recovery

National and international organisations supported affected communities through relief assistance, healthcare and recovery programmes.

AFTER THE STORM

The Road to Recovery

In the months and years following the disaster, national and international organisations supported relief and recovery efforts. These efforts included providing food, clean water, emergency shelter and healthcare, as well as helping affected communities rebuild their homes and livelihoods.

Cyclone Nargis remains an important event in Myanmar's history. Its legacy is remembered through the experiences of survivors and the continuing efforts to improve disaster preparedness and protect communities from future cyclones.

LOOKING TOWARDS THE FUTURE

Lessons We Can Learn

Cyclone Nargis highlights why communities need reliable information, proper preparation and effective disaster response systems before a cyclone occurs.

Early Warning Systems

Timely and accessible warnings help people understand approaching dangers and make informed decisions.

Disaster Preparedness

Emergency plans, essential supplies and knowledge of safe shelters can help communities prepare.

Community Awareness

Sharing reliable information and learning together can strengthen community readiness and safety.

STORMORA • REMEMBER AND PREPARE

Understanding the Past. Preparing for the Future.

Cyclone Nargis reminds us that disaster preparedness is essential, not optional. By learning from past events, improving early warning systems and encouraging community awareness, we can help reduce future risks and build safer, more resilient communities.
