OUR PROJECT INITIATIVE

Our Course of Action

As Year 9 students, we aim to create an educational short film about cyclone early warning systems and disaster preparedness. The film will show how teenagers may struggle during a cyclone when they are unprepared, while teaching viewers about cyclone warnings, emergency preparations and evacuation safety.

Our Educational Short Film

Using storytelling and practical information, our film aims to make cyclone safety easier for teenagers to understand and apply.
