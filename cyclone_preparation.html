Myanmar • Disaster Preparedness

The Next Cyclone Should Not Catch Us Unprepared

Cyclone Nargis showed how devastating a cyclone can be for Myanmar. We cannot stop a cyclone from forming, but we can prepare our families and communities before it arrives.

Key Focus

Early warnings, evacuation routes, and community preparedness.

Impact

Protecting households, saving lives, and reducing severe risks.

LEARN FROM HISTORY

Cyclone Nargis: A Lesson for Myanmar

On 2-3 May 2008, Cyclone Nargis struck Myanmar, causing severe destruction, especially in the Ayeyarwady Delta. Its impacts show why early warnings, evacuation plans and community preparedness are important.

What happened?

Nargis brought destructive winds, heavy rain and a storm surge. Many communities lost family members, homes and their sources of income.

The disaster also showed that weather information must reach people clearly and early enough for them to take action.

What can we learn from Nargis?

1. People need to understand cyclone warnings and take them seriously.

2. Families should know where to go if their area becomes dangerous.

3. Emergency supplies should be prepared before a cyclone approaches.

4. Communities should share reliable information and help people who may need assistance to evacuate.

PLAN BEFORE THE STORM

Your Preparation Timeline

Preparation should begin before dangerous weather arrives. Follow official instructions throughout every stage.

01

Before Cyclone Season

Find out whether your community is at risk of flooding or storm surge. Identify a safe shelter, learn evacuation routes and agree on a family meeting place.

02

When a Cyclone Warning Is Issued

Check official forecasts from Myanmar's Department of Meteorology and Hydrology (DMH). Prepare your emergency bag, charge phones and secure loose objects if conditions are safe.

03

When Evacuation Is Advised

Leave promptly using the recommended route and go to the designated safe shelter. Do not wait for water to rise or travel through floodwater.

PACK ESSENTIALS

What Should Be in Your Emergency Bag?

Keep these items together so your family can find them quickly. Store food and water safely, and prepare supplies for your family's needs.

💧

Water and Food

Safe drinking water and food that does not spoil quickly.

🩹

Health Supplies

First-aid kit, necessary medicines and hygiene items.

🔦

Light and Power

Flashlight, spare batteries and a charged power bank.

📻

Weather Information

Battery-powered radio and a written list of important contact numbers.

📄

Important Documents

Identification and important papers in waterproof packaging.

👕

Clothing

A change of clothes and essential personal items.
