KNOW THE WARNING.
STAY SAFE.

Early warnings give people time to prepare before a cyclone arrives. Knowing where to get reliable information can help protect families and communities across Myanmar.

What Is a Cyclone Early Warning System?

An early warning system detects dangerous weather and shares information before a cyclone reaches an area. It helps people understand the risk and prepare for possible danger.

01. Monitor

Weather experts use satellites, weather observations and forecasts to monitor developing cyclones.

02. Warn

Warning information is shared through television, radio, official websites, social media and local authorities.

03. Prepare

People follow official advice, prepare emergency supplies and move to safer places when instructed.

Remember: Time to Prepare Saves Lives

In Myanmar, check cyclone forecasts and official warnings from the Department of Meteorology and Hydrology (DMH). Do not depend on rumours or unverified social media posts.

Warning Signs to Watch For

Strong Winds

Winds may become stronger as a cyclone approaches. Stay indoors and secure loose objects when it is safe.

Heavy Rain

Heavy rainfall can cause flooding and make roads dangerous.

Rising Sea Water

Coastal areas may experience storm surges. Follow evacuation instructions and stay away from the shore.

Important: Don't wait to see these signs before preparing. A cyclone may arrive before you notice obvious changes in the weather.

What Should You Do When a Warning Is Issued?

✓ Check the latest official weather updates.

✓ Tell your family and discuss your emergency plan.

✓ Prepare drinking water, food, medicine and a flashlight.

✓ Charge your phone and keep important documents safe.

✓ Follow evacuation instructions from local authorities.

✓ Never ignore warnings or share unverified information.
