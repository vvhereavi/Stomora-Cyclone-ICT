Our Mission

Our mission is to raise awareness about cyclone risks and strengthen disaster preparedness in Myanmar's coastal communities. Through Stormora, we aim to provide reliable information about cyclones, explain the importance of early warning systems, and share practical safety guidelines. We also hope to educate younger generations so they can recognize potential dangers, respond appropriately to warnings, and take the necessary steps to protect themselves, their families, and their communities.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create safer and more resilient coastal communities where everyone has the knowledge and confidence to prepare for and respond to cyclones. We hope to build a future where reliable warnings reach everyone, disaster preparedness becomes a shared responsibility, and people understand how to reduce risks before disasters occur. By encouraging awareness and preparedness among younger generations, we aim to contribute to a safer future for communities across Myanmar.
